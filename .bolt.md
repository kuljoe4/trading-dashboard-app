## 2026-05-28 - Risk Aggregation Optimization
**Learning:** Recalculating absolute risk in hot loops for every active trade is inefficient when the data is relatively static.
**Action:** Persist `risk_usdt` on the trade object and update it only when SL changes, allowing O(1) access during metrics aggregation.

## 2026-05-29 - Cockpit Metrics Optimization
**Learning:** Redundant filtering and aggregation of large historical trade sets in the high-frequency tick loop causes unnecessary GC pressure and CPU spikes.
**Action:** Implement lazy-loaded caching for closed trade stats and single-pass O(N+M) aggregation for active variants. Always calculate global PnL from the full active trade set to ensure accuracy during delta broadcasts.
## 2026-05-30 - [Scanner Short-Circuit]
**Learning:** Performing full signal and score evaluations (volatility, trend) on a large watchlist (50+ symbols) every few seconds is a major CPU sink. Most symbols are inactive (low momentum) at any given time.
**Action:** Implement an early return in the scanner loop for symbols below the configured momentum threshold to achieve a high-efficiency 'gating' mechanism for technical analysis.
