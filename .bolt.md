## 2026-05-30 - Config Modal Mobile UX
**Learning:** Mobile browsers (especially iOS Safari) often fail to recalibrate the viewport correctly after a virtual keyboard is hidden if the DOM structure is complex or uses fixed/absolute positioning within a drawer. Sticky headers with backdrop-blur can also cause layout shifts during keyboard transitions.
**Action:** Always use 'inputMode' for optimal keyboards, 'scrollIntoView' on focus, and a 'window.scrollTo' hack on blur to force a layout recalc. Use 'data-vaul-no-drag' on scrollable areas within drawers to prevent gesture conflicts.
