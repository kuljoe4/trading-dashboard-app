feat(ui): add Martingale badge and tooltips to active and historical trades

This commit adds a visual indicator for trades that were opened under a Martingale recovery strategy.

* Backend (`execution.service.ts` & `riskEngine.ts`): RiskEngine now explicitly returns the current martingale step. ExecutionService embeds this step and multiplier into the `trade.strategy_config` (`is_martingale`, `martingale_multiplier`, `martingale_step`).
* Frontend (`ActiveTradeCard.jsx`): Shows a distinct 'MARTINGALE (Nx)' badge with tooltips showing the current recovery step.
* Frontend (`HistoryView.jsx` & `TradeDetailContent.jsx`): Adds clear martingale indicator badges for historical records.

Resolves user request for martingale visibility.
